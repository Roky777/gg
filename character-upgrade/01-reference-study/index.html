02 · Target model guide

Coordinates refer to the original 512 × 640 source canvas. Yellow points are the existing cutout rig’s anchor candidates, to be validated during refitting.

Face identity
Match flame perimeter, eye whites and pupils, brow arcs, small nose and gentle smile. Preserve their relative positions.
Childlike silhouette
The head occupies about 52.4% of the source’s visible height. Short torso, open shoulders, large gloves and chunky shoes define the target.
Outfit identity
Rich blue sports shirt; small circular chest badge; blue shorts with white side stripes; white gloves and socks; blue trainers.
Join construction
Neck below head; arm roots beneath sleeves; wrists beneath glove cuffs; thigh roots beneath shorts; ankles beneath socks/shoes. Match paint at the overlap.
Contour treatment
Dark, crisp outside outlines. Feathering belongs only on concealed overlap edges. Visible cuffs and hems remain intentional edges.

05 · What the Lucas construction tells us

Original Lucas atlas, inspected alongside its binary skeleton. Transparent packing is shown on the dark panel.

More than one cut per limb
The atlas includes alternative attachment shapes and hand/mouth poses. A neutral reference alone cannot replace every gesture.
Meshes and weights do the bending
The inspected skeleton has 65 weighted mesh attachments. Repainting its texture cannot, by itself, change its joint geometry correctly.
Soft hidden ends, clear visible forms
Some part ends fade into matching paint. Keep that technique inside overlaps; do not apply a blur around the whole limb.
Protect draw order and masks
Six clipping attachments and animated attachment swaps are present. Facial layers and prop contacts need individual checks.

06 · Acceptance gates for the next stages

Appearance

Aligned neutral silhouette, face landmarks, outfit details and colour reviewed together. No unsupported “95% achieved” claim.

Construction

Refit only the necessary setup geometry, pivots, meshes and weights. Test the exposed and hidden joins through bends.

Motion

Review idle, speech, yes/no and celebration before the full library. Then check contacts, props, locomotion and transitions.

The available reference actor is 236 × 403 pixels at native visible size. Side/back artwork and alternate hand gestures are missing. Some clean face paint was reconstructed in the existing extraction. This study establishes the target; it does not certify final artwork or animation quality.
