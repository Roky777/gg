Sparky voice recording list — Hinglish

165 proposed recording files: 52 prompts/reactions, 26 letter-and-word readings, 11 number readings, and 76 picture names. This is a recording plan, not an audio implementation or a claim that these recordings already exist.

Performance: warm, playful young companion; ordinary conversational Hindi with familiar English game words. Clear pronunciation, a smile, no shouting or artificial baby voice. Text is in Roman Hinglish for recording. English letter names, not phonics sounds, for A–Z. Keep completion takes around 1–2 seconds so the existing 2.5-second next-board flow can finish naturally; this is a production target, not a research threshold. Record each complete line separately with clean starts and ends.

Playback: one voice at a time. First-use instruction, then let the child think. Hints on request or repeated difficulty. Counting only when the child taps an uncounted object. Correct moves usually get a gesture/sound; one short line for a completed board. Longer letter/picture review only on request, with auto-advance paused until review ends. Do not splice syllables, number inflections or sentence fragments to imitate conversational speech. If a natural full sentence is needed later, record it as a complete take.

Sources informing the approach (not validation of these particular lines):
- EEF, Developing vocabulary in early years education: https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/early-years/literacy/developing-vocabulary-in-early-years-education/ — quality of language interaction, guided interaction, responsive support.
- NAEYC/Fred Rogers Center position statement: https://www.naeyc.org/resources/topics/technology-and-media-0/technology-and-interactive-media-position-statement — intentional, developmentally appropriate interactive media.

The 40 existing assembly facts are optional enrichment and should not auto-play after every solve. This pack covers all five current game mechanics and the names of their pictures without needing those longer facts. No addition/subtraction recordings are required.
